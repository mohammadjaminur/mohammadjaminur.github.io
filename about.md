  <h1>About me</h1>
  <p class="lede">I'm a Computer Science Ph.D. student at UC Riverside, advised by Dr.&nbsp;Michalis Faloutsos. My current research focuses on selective Internet measurement. I combine active and passive DNS history with server fingerprints to rank hosts for active probing and identify likely malware command-and-control (C2) infrastructure.</p>

  <p>Earlier in my Ph.D., I worked with Dr.&nbsp;Shaolei Ren on scheduling AI workloads across time and location to reduce data centers' energy, water, carbon, and public-health costs. Before UCR, I completed an M.S. at Western Michigan University in 2021. My M.S. research with Dr.&nbsp;Shameek Bhattacharjee focused on incident and attack detection in transportation and smart-grid systems, including detection under data-poisoning attacks. I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BUET in 2016, then spent three years as a Software Engineer at Reve Systems in Dhaka.</p>

  <p>I'm seeking academic and industry research positions starting in 2027, including postdoctoral, AI Engineer, and Applied Scientist roles. I am particularly interested in Internet-scale measurement, large-scale anomaly detection, and the security of deployed machine-learning systems.</p>
